Pocket Guitar

Hold your phone sideways. Press frets with your left fingers to shape a chord, then strum or pick on the right. While a string rings you can hammer onto a higher fret or slide your finger along it. Tap and hold a BARRE button above a fret to lay a full barre across the strings — drag it sideways to change frets, drag it toward the high-e string to shrink it, let go to remove it. Use the slider by your strumming hand to move up and down the neck.
