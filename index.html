Pocket Guitar

Hold your phone sideways. Hold down frets with your left fingers (a few at once) to shape a chord, then strum or pick the strings on the right. Let go and the string rings open. Strum faster to play louder.

📱

Turn sideways

Pocket Guitar plays in landscape, like holding a real neck. Rotate your phone to play. (Tip: turn off rotation lock in Control Center.)
